FAQ

How many client domains can I monitor?

The Agency plan covers up to 25 domains on one plan, so you can watch every active client domain from a single dashboard.

What are white-label reports?

White-label reports are deliverability reports branded to your agency instead of Inboxproof. Share a client's score, the broken records, and the exact fix under your own name.

Do I need to change my clients' DNS to monitor a domain?

No. Inboxproof queries live DNS read-only. No account, no DNS changes, nothing to install on the client side.
