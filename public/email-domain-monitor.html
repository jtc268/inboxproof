Coverage

What gets checked.

SPF, DKIM and DMARC records, plus MX routing, STARTTLS, reverse DNS and blocklist lookups for observed mail-server addresses.

These are configuration checks. They do not send test messages or prove inbox placement. Custom DKIM selectors can be missed, and blocked network probes are reported as unverified.

Before you start

A few practical details.

Do I need to change DNS or install anything?

No. Inboxproof reads public records and probes accessible mail-server settings. You review and apply any changes with your provider.

How quickly will I hear about a problem?

Monitoring runs daily. Alerts follow a detected regression during a scheduled check. You can also request a recheck from your dashboard.

How do I access the dashboard after paying?

Open Sign in, enter the email used at checkout, and use the one-time link sent to that address. No password is required.

Can I manage several clients?

Agency covers up to 25 domains for $49.50/month. See the Agency plan for details.

How do I cancel?

Use Manage billing in your signed-in dashboard to open the billing portal and manage your subscription.
