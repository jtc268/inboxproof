FAQ

SaaS questions, answered.

Do I need to change my DNS to run an audit?

No. The free audit queries live DNS read-only. No account, no DNS changes, nothing to install.

What does a broken SPF record do to my SaaS product email?

If your SPF record is missing or misconfigured, mailbox providers like Gmail and Outlook treat your domain as unverified. Onboarding emails, password resets and receipts can land in spam or get rejected outright.

How often should I re-check my domain?

Email providers change sending IPs and DNS providers rotate records. The Pro plan re-audits up to 5 domains every 24 hours and emails you the moment a record breaks.

Is the audit really free?

Yes. The full 7-check audit (MX, SPF, DKIM, DMARC, TLS, PTR, blocklists) is free and needs no account. Pro adds daily monitoring, score history and alerts.
