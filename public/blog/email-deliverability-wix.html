Email deliverability for Wix: keep your form emails out of spam

Wix sites lose leads when contact form emails and password resets land in the spam folder. The visitor does not get the confirmation, gets confused, and does not come back. The fix is usually a DNS record, not a template change.

Why Wix email goes to spam

Wix sends email through its own mail server by default. This is different from WordPress, where your hosting provider handles email. Wix's mail server has a good reputation, but the problem is that your domain's DNS records may not be configured to match.

The receiving mail server checks your domain's DNS records and the sending IP's reputation. If your domain does not have the correct SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records pointing to Wix's mail server, the email gets filtered to spam.

The frustrating part: Wix's email log shows "delivered." The email left Wix's server. It just did not reach the inbox because your domain's DNS records do not match.

The 6 records that decide delivery

Here is what the receiving mail server checks, in order:

  1. MX record: tells the mail server where to deliver mail for your domain. If this is missing, email bounces immediately.
  2. SPF record: lists the IP addresses and services allowed to send email as your domain. If Wix's IP is not listed, the email fails SPF.
  3. DKIM record: a cryptographic signature that proves the email was not tampered with in transit. If the signature is broken or missing, the email fails DKIM.
  4. DMARC record: tells the receiving server what to do when SPF or DKIM fails. Without a DMARC record, Gmail and Outlook apply their own (stricter) policy.
  5. TLS/STARTTLS: encrypts the email in transit. If your mail server does not support TLS, some providers downgrade or reject the email.
  6. PTR / reverse DNS: maps the sending IP back to a hostname. If the PTR does not match the sending server, the email gets flagged.

Any one of these can break your deliverability. Most Wix sites have at least one missing or misconfigured.

The Wix-specific gotchas

1. You are using a custom domain but the DNS records are not set up

If you use a custom domain on Wix (e.g., yoursite.com instead of yoursite.wixsite.com), you need to add Wix's DNS records to your domain's DNS settings. If these records are missing, your email will fail SPF and DKIM.

Fix: check your domain's DNS settings and add Wix's SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records. Wix provides the exact record values in your account settings.

2. Your SPF record does not include Wix

If you use a custom domain on Wix, your SPF record needs to include Wix's mail server. For example, your SPF record should include "include:wixdns.com". If this include is missing, your email will fail SPF.

Fix: check your current SPF record and add the Wix include. If you use multiple services, add all of their includes.

3. Your DKIM record is missing

If you use a custom domain on Wix, you need to add Wix's DKIM record to your DNS. Wix provides a specific DKIM record that you need to add to your DNS. If the DKIM record is missing, the email fails DKIM.

Fix: check your Wix account settings for the DKIM record and add it to your DNS.

4. Your DMARC record is missing

Gmail has been enforcing DMARC more strictly since early 2024. A domain with no DMARC record is treated as unauthenticated. Gmail filters these emails more aggressively. Set your DMARC record to at least p=quarantine, and ideally p=reject once you are confident your SPF and DKIM are correct.

Fix: add a DMARC record to your DNS. Start with p=none, monitor the reports, then move to p=quarantine and p=reject.

5. You are using Wix's email marketing feature

If you use Wix's built-in email marketing feature, your email will be sent from Wix's mail server. This is different from your domain's mail server. You need to make sure your domain's DNS records are configured to allow Wix to send email on your behalf.

Fix: check your Wix account settings for the email marketing DNS records and add them to your DNS.

Quick checklist

  • MX record exists and points to a valid mail server
  • SPF record includes Wix's mail server (include:wixdns.com)
  • DKIM record is present and the selector matches Wix's DKIM
  • DMARC record exists with p=quarantine or p=reject
  • TLS/STARTTLS is enabled on your mail server
  • PTR record matches your sending IP
  • Your From: email address is on your domain
