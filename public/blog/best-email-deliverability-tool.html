Best email deliverability tools in 2026, compared

Most "email deliverability tools" are really one of four things: a one-off domain checker, a monitoring service, a DMARC platform, or a cold email system. They solve different problems, and picking the wrong one is why most teams pay for a tool that does not answer their question. Here is the honest breakdown by use case.

One-off domain checks

If you need to know right now whether your SPF, DKIM, DMARC, MX and TLS records are sane, you want a free audit tool. Inboxproof runs seven live checks on a domain in about 30 seconds with no signup: SPF syntax and lookup count, DKIM presence, DMARC policy, MX reachability, TLS, and IP reputation. MXToolbox and the Google Postmaster Tools dashboard cover parts of the same ground, but they are check-by-check rather than one scored report you can share.

Monitoring

Records break. A DNS change, an expired DKIM key, or a new sender that bypasses your SPF will silently start sending you to spam. A monitoring service re-checks your domain on a schedule and alerts you when something changes. Inboxproof Pro checks your domain daily and emails you the moment a check flips. Valimail and dmarcian are the established paid options if you need enterprise-grade DMARC aggregate reporting on top.

DMARC platforms

If you get DMARC aggregate reports (RUFUS) and need to parse them, find spoofing attempts, and move your policy from p=none to p=reject, you want a dedicated DMARC platform. dmarcian and Valimail are the two names that come up most. Inboxproof covers the record side: it tells you whether your DMARC policy exists, what it does, and whether your subdomains are protected.

Cold email systems

Cold outreach needs its own stack: dedicated sending domains, warmup, and rotation. Instantly, Smartlead and similar tools handle the sending side. What they do not check is whether your sending domain is actually configured to pass authentication, which is the difference between your campaigns hitting the inbox and hitting the spam folder or getting hard rejected. Run the free audit on each sending domain before you start warmup.

How to choose

Start with the question you are trying to answer. "Is my domain configured correctly?" is a free audit. "Did something break this week?" is monitoring. "Why am I still getting spoofed?" is a DMARC platform. "Why is my outreach bouncing?" is a cold email tool plus an audit of the sending domains it uses. Most teams need the first two, and both are cheap or free to start.
