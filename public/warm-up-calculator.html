Warm-up checklist

  • Send from a secondary domain (e.g. getyourbrand.com), never your brand domain.
  • Grow slowly. A smooth ramp over weeks beats a sudden jump. The schedule below does the math.
  • Keep bounce under 0.5% and complaints near zero. If they climb, pause and clean your list.
  • One recipient per email, a real reply-to, plain text. No heavy HTML or images early on.
  • Check your domain first. A clean SPF/DKIM/DMARC and IP reputation is the foundation the ramp builds on.
