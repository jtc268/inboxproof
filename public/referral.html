Your referral link

How it works, in detail

Your link looks like inboxproof.email/?ref=ABC123. When someone runs an audit from that link, we quietly attach your code to their audit. Nothing is shown to them, and no account is needed. If they later upgrade to Pro, your account gets a free month added to it.

If you are already on Pro, the month stacks: when your paid subscription ends, your account stays Pro until the earned window runs out. If you are not on Pro yet, the first earned month activates Pro for you right away, for 30 days.

It works for agencies the same way it works for individuals. Share your link with a client whose domain is failing, let them run the free audit, and if they bring their monitoring in-house with Pro, you keep a month of your own.

Questions

Do the people I refer know I sent them the link?

No. The link just gives them the same free audit anyone gets. Referral tracking happens on our side, and we never show it to them.

What counts as a referral that earns a month?

A person who ran an audit through your link and then upgrades to Pro. Free audits alone do not earn months, because the program rewards the people who bring a paying customer.

Does it work for the Agency plan?

Yes. If a referral upgrades to Agency, you still earn a full month of Pro. The reward is the same for any paid upgrade.

Can I refer myself?

No. A link does not count against the account that owns it.

How long does an earned month last?

30 days from when the referral upgrades. Multiple referrals stack, and the window extends with each one.
