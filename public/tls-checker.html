TLS / STARTTLS checker

Find out whether your mail server encrypts email in transit. We connect to each MX host on port 25, check for STARTTLS, and verify the certificate. Free, no signup.

What a good result looks like

  • STARTTLS offered on every MX host you actually use.
  • Certificate valid and not expiring within the next two weeks.
  • Issuer is a trusted CA (Let's Encrypt, DigiCert, etc.).
  • No plaintext fallback for your real traffic.
