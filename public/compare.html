Who each tool is for

Inboxproof

For small teams and solo founders who want a clear score, a plain-English fix list, and daily monitoring without enterprise pricing. The free checker is the fastest way to see your domain's current state, and Pro adds monitoring and alerts.

MXToolbox

A broad DNS and email diagnostics suite. Great for one-off lookups and network diagnostics, but the monitoring and DMARC features are split across paid tiers and the UI is dense for non-experts.

GlockApps

A solid DMARC-focused platform with good reporting. Priced for mid-market teams; the free tier is limited and the full value is in the paid plans.

dmarcian

A DMARC-first platform with strong aggregate-report parsing. Aimed at teams already deep in DMARC; the entry price is higher and the tooling assumes you know the space.

Why small teams pick Inboxproof

  • Shareable reports. Every audit produces a public link you can send to a client or teammate.
