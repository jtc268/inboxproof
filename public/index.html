Before you start

A few useful details.

Will this change my DNS?

No. The audit reads public records and probes accessible mail servers. You or your provider makes any changes after reviewing the findings.

Does a good score guarantee inbox delivery?

No. The score summarizes configuration checks. Reputation, recipient feedback, message content, and provider decisions also affect delivery. Inboxproof does not send placement-test messages.

Can this verify a message’s DKIM signature?

The domain check searches common selectors for published keys. To inspect the signature and alignment on an actual message, use our email header analyzer.

What happens if I add my email?

Email is optional. Add it to save a report to your account. Paid monitoring sends updates from scheduled checks. See the privacy page for data handling.

How is the score calculated?

Each check has a fixed weight. A pass earns its full weight, a warning earns half, and a failure earns zero. Review the individual findings alongside the total.
